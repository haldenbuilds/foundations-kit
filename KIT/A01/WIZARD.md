# A01 operating stages

Standard: `Wizard_Build_Standard_v1`, provisional v0.9. Build id: `a01`. Status: staged working instructions. The owner folder is the root for all runtime records below; the A01 folder remains the instruction source.

## Scope classification

A01 is a repeatable package. The recipient's agent interviews, interprets evidence, and drafts a plan. It takes owner records to a saved audit and a continuing plan. All scope predicates apply.

## WB-1 · Interview and ratify

Use ONBOARDING.md's versioned ten-question schema. Preserve raw answers and unknowns in SCALE-INTAKE.md; derive SCALE-BRIEF.md with outcome, intended user, constraints, unknowns, and success evidence. Show the exact brief. The owner ratifies or revises it. Save subject version or digest, actor, words, decision, and time in DECISIONS.md as G1. Stop dependent generation if the decision is absent. A target is not a promise.

## WB-2 · Audit, sweep, and choose

From Q4, declare roots and artifact classes in ASSETS.md before inspection: sales records, offer terms, delivery/time records, cash history, dated obligations, and existing plans. Sweep only those roots and classes. Record every candidate's path or stable ID, role, origin, date, digest when available, conflicts, and uncertainty. Test access with an owner-named known file: finding it supports a successful sweep; inability to read it is UNAVAILABLE, not no files found. Record exact scope and procedure so the sweep can be repeated. Present candidates for USE, REPLACE, REFERENCE, or IGNORE. Save owner dispositions and inventory version in DECISIONS.md as G2 before treating a candidate as authoritative. REPLACE is a disposition, not permission to overwrite. Preserve existing material before any approved edit.

## WB-3 · Assess needed versus PARK

List every discovered and proposed capability in SCOPE.md. Ask the owner to classify each NEEDED-NOW, PARK, or REJECT, with reason and owner. Each PARK row keeps a stable ID, source, dependencies, and wake condition or ON-DEMAND. Save the scope version and decision as G3 in DECISIONS.md. Only NEEDED-NOW enters the current plan. `Show parked work` reads this register at any later session. Never activate a parked item from elapsed time alone.

## WB-4 · Recommend research

In RESEARCH.md consider advisor evidence, current practice, and established practice separately for the open decisions. Each material recommendation records question, proposed source or advisor type, recency need, decision use, access limit, and owner accept, decline, or park decision. If a class has no relevant open question, state why. Research is optional; record unavailable channels honestly. Acceptance of a proposal does not grant network or account access. When evidence is obtained within permission, record source and retrieval date, distinguish it from inference, and ask the owner before changing the brief. Save the recommendation version and dispositions as G4 in DECISIONS.md. Do not invent best practice when no evidence was retrieved.

## WB-5 · Guide and callable manual

Use OPERATOR-GUIDE.md and AGENT-GUIDE.md, then the audit template and ONGOING.md. Write GUIDE-COVERAGE.md in the owner folder: each supported question, guide section, answer, and outcome. Test `How do I resume?` against ONGOING.md and a question the guides cannot answer against the UNKNOWN-IN-GUIDES rule. Save the audit draft and review its evidence. The owner adopts or revises the exact plan version; save that decision as G5 in DECISIONS.md. Adoption permits only the acts already within the recorded authority. Consequential acts still need exact approval.

## Operator approval boundary

The owner owns G1 brief ratification, G2 asset choices, G3 scope choices, G4 research adoption, and G5 plan adoption. Every receipt has actor, exact subject version or digest, decision, words, and time. Silence and a passing check never approve anything. Materially changed subjects invalidate their old approval. A prior approval of these exact subjects can be referenced; do not ask again without a changed subject.

## Three-version practice

This package is the commissioned light advisor with a callable guide. No other variant is required to use it. A version choice is the owner's decision.

## Receipts

SCALE-INTAKE.md, SCALE-BRIEF.md, ASSETS.md, SCOPE.md, RESEARCH.md, GUIDE-COVERAGE.md, and DECISIONS.md preserve the stages. SCALE-PLAN.md, NUMBERS.md, TASKS.md, RISKS.md, and SESSION.md preserve ongoing work. Until created, these are output contracts, not claims that approvals exist. Do not ship populated owner records inside A01.
